# The mountain's recordings

Everything here is public domain or CC0: no attribution is required, but this is where each recording came from.
The build hashes each file on its own (`/assets/sound/<name>-<hash>.mp3`); `src/mountain/audio.js` plays them.
The ambient files are cut from the sources at MP3 frame boundaries (no re-encoding); the music is the whole piece.
